AI stock rout pushes S&P 500 into correction; Nasdaq negative for 2026

The S&P 500 fell 3.7% on Friday to 6,910.39, more than 10% below its Oct. 6 high. That was its worst week since April 2025 and a seven-day losing streak. The Nasdaq dropped 5.3% and is now down for the year, and the Dow fell 1.9%. Chip stocks led the fall: Nvidia fell 13.8%, Micron 13.9%, AMD 13.3% and Broadcom 12.1%. How to study a real WSOP final table hand by hand in GTO Wizard

The method is to open the Events tab, pick a real final table (here the 2024 $25k Super High Roller), and watch each hand's timestamped stream. Before each hand, guess the action from the preflop hole cards, then compare it to the ICM solution and note what surprised you. The session shows ICM ranges are more top-heavy than chip EV ranges, with suited connectors dropping out. The reason is that small hands win through big pots, and big pots carry more ICM tax. The big stack's early-position open is about 30% under ICM versus 20% in chip EV at the same stacks. Bubble factors show the pressure between each pair: low jack versus button was 2.24, against 1.35 for the button versus low jack. Short stacks feel less ICM pressure because they are likely to bust next anyway. Mixed solver strategies, like 5s shoving or folding, exist partly to keep opponents indifferent.

  • Min-raise suited aces that keep draw value and implied odds, and shove offsuit aces like A8, which have poor drawing equity.
  • The breakdown and reports tabs are post-flop only.

Epictetus asked: who is your master? Seneca said everyone serves something

Epictetus spent 30 years enslaved. He later asked students who their master was, having watched powerful men in Nero's court grovel to a shoemaker and beg for money. Seneca's version is that everyone is a slave to something such as ambition or appetite. He said of the general Marius that 'ambition commanded Marius'. The point of discipline is self-ownership. The master can be a job, a schedule, an addiction or a compulsion.

Saylor: Strategy has 3.4 years of cash and sees only a Bitcoin collapse to zero as existential

In an investor Q&A, Saylor and CEO Phong Le said Strategy holds about 3.4 years of USD reserves, up from 0.7 years when Bitcoin was at $62,000. They said that would cover dividends and interest even if it could not sell any securities for three years. They describe the business as issuing digital credit, with common shareholders keeping the spread above a roughly 10.5% hurdle rate. Gross Bitcoin per share matters to long-horizon bulls, and net Bitcoin per share is the test of whether a swap helps shareholders immediately. On the short-duration preferred STRC, they say credit markets have contracted for about six months. Strategy bought back $3B of STRC below $100, and Saylor guesses organic demand at the 12% dividend is $5B to $7.5B. They say they buy it back below par and issue it above par. Saylor says they will not raise the dividend to chase growth, because that would raise the hurdle rate and the risk to equity holders.

  • Capital structure cited: about $6.7B of convertibles, $12.7B of preferreds, $52.5B in Bitcoin reserves, and roughly 850,000 BTC held.
  • Swap thresholds: selling MSTR for dollars is accretive above 1x mNAV, and selling MSTR for BTC at roughly 1.3x.
  • Saylor calls STRC investment grade, about 5x over-collateralized, with a fair spread of 60 to 70 bps.

Anthropic mathematician claims Claude helped prove the Riemann hypothesis

Levent Alpöge says he spent about six weeks using a non-public version of Claude to produce a proof of the Riemann hypothesis. The Lean formalization is over 250,000 pages. Terence Tao said checking it is still some way off, though a human author is available to help. Earlier AI maths claims, such as OpenAI's Navier-Stokes result, remain unconfirmed. A group of mathematicians has criticized firms for advertising such claims. Treat it as a claim, not a result, until experts review it.

Daily Stoic reading · Wish only what is yours

If you wish your children and your wife and your friends to live forever, you are foolish, for you wish things to be in your power which are not so, and what belongs to others to be your own. So likewise, if you wish your servant to be without fault, you are foolish, for you wish vice not to be vice but something else. But if you wish not to be disappointed in your desires, that is in your own power. Exercise, therefore, what is in your power. A man’s master is he who is able to confer or remove whatever that man seeks or shuns. Whoever then would be free, let him wish nothing, let him decline nothing, which depends on others; else he must necessarily be a slave.

— Epictetus, Enchiridion

In plain terms: you cannot make your children, spouse, or friends live forever, and you cannot make a servant flawless, so wanting those outcomes sets you up for disappointment; the reliable lever is your own desires, which you can shape. For poker and other uncertain play, this points to judging decisions by the quality of your process rather than by the outcome a hand or session delivers, since card distribution and opponents' choices belong to others. Treat a bad beat the way you would treat a child's illness you cannot control: do what is yours to do, then let the result go.

Mental model · Psychology

Commitment and Consistency

Once we commit publicly, we distort reality to stay consistent. Cults exploit this with small initial commitments (a meeting, a donation) that escalate. Munger's rule: be very careful what you commit to publicly. Once you've said it, your brain will defend it against evidence.
