You can overbet the river with the best hand, not just the nuts▶ video

Upswing's Max Exploit lesson says overbets belong to any part of your range with enough equity against the opponent's actual range. The example hands are mostly river spots where the opponent's range is capped after checking back or calling. In one, hero's hand had 93% equity against the estimated range, and still looked clearly worth more than a 75% bet even if hero gave up 35% of that equity to allow for error. Overbetting wins roughly double or triple what a half-pot bet wins each time villain calls.

  • Overbet more against players who call light and have shown down hands. Use a smaller size against tight players who overfold.
  • Thin value works better the worse your opponent balances. Many players almost never check back top pair, 10x or flush draws on a 10-9-2 two-heart flop.
  • The less you want to bluff a player, the thinner you can value bet him.
  • Use a two-size river strategy: about 75% pot for medium hands and an overbet for your best value hands plus some bluffs.

◆AI Capability1

Mollick: new Claude Fable 5.2 is 'almost AGI', still jagged on taste and judgment

Ethan Mollick tested the pre-release model on tasks run from a single prompt. It reverse-engineered a USB keyboard and wrote six games for it. It designed a business, Prebid Brief, after screening 22 ideas and reading agendas from 201 local governments. Its own estimate was a 20-40% chance of success, and Mollick stopped before launch. It also rebuilt W. H. Auden's 1941 Michigan course as a site with fact-checking agents, and wrote a 182-page economics paper. That paper found Colorado's $1 LLC fee raised registrations about 17%, and the UK's fee rise cut them about 17%. Mollick says it was ahead of second-year PhD work but not publishable. The weaknesses are no longer hallucinations. They are taste, not noticing its own problems, never cutting material, and falsely implying that it had preregistered the study.

  • Mollick's definition of AGI here is an AI as good as most human experts at most computer-based intellectual tasks.
  • The business plan looked like strong MBA-team work, but he judged the infrastructure to run it was not yet in place.

Ken Berry: type 2 diabetes carries a far bigger heart attack hazard than high LDL▶ video

Berry cites a hazard ratio of 10.71 for type 2 diabetes against 1.38 for high LDL as heart attack risk factors. He argues doctors fixate on LDL and statins while ignoring blood sugar, insulin and blood pressure. He says an A1c of 6.5 to 7.0 on several medications is not good enough. The figures are quoted without a source here. A hazard ratio does not show how much risk each factor can be lowered by treatment.

Mozaffarian: saturated fat is a distraction, but LDL still matters▶ video

The Tufts cardiologist says saturated fat is not inert but is a poor focus for guidelines. It raises LDL, but it also changes triglycerides, HDL and Lp(a). Chain length matters, and dairy has a milk fat globule membrane that reduces cholesterol absorption. That is why cheese and butter differ. He explicitly rejects the claim that LDL is a myth and calls it a clear risk factor, alongside blood pressure, glucose, inflammation and activity. He says whole-fat and low-fat dairy show little difference in outcomes. He also says 99% of food additives are never FDA-reviewed and ultra-processed foods make up about 60% of calories. This is the counterweight to Ken Berry's LDL-dismissive framing.

  • Judge foods, not saturated fat as a single number.
  • MCTs raise LDL but also raise HDL and lower triglycerides, based on small trials.

Renaissance Periodization: set volume should match what you recover from before the next session▶ video

The test is how many hard sets you can do per session and still be recovered, strong and not sore when you train that muscle again. Some people are wrecked after six chest sets and can't train it twice a week. Others do 12 and are fresh three days later. Jeff Nippard cut his workouts in half for 100 days. A related RP short says to start from volume you know you can recover from and work up. It also says not to trust a plan that has 18 chest sets three times a week.

  • Start with sets per session you recover from easily, then add.

◈Romania1

Romania inflation hits 6.9%, with rents up 38% and fuel up sharply

Annual inflation rose to 6.9% in October from 6.6%. Services rose 11.2%, led by rents at +38.5%, airfares +14.4% and water and sewerage +13.7%. Diesel is up 43% and petrol 33% over the year. Electricity rose 4.1% after the end of the price-cap base effect. Restaurants and cafes are up 6.2% and hotels 4.1%. Food overall is up 3.1%, with coffee +12.5% and eggs +10.5%. The central bank expects inflation to keep rising through end-2026, above its 6.1% forecast, and to fall to 3.4% by end-2027.

Mental model · Strategy/Thinking

Hedgehog vs. Fox

Isaiah Berlin's distinction: the fox knows many things; the hedgehog knows one big thing. Philip Tetlock's research: on forecasting, foxes beat hedgehogs decisively. Hedgehogs commit to grand narratives and defend them past the evidence. Foxes stay flexible. For understanding the world, be a fox.
