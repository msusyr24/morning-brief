NPR News

Iran attacks US radar in Bahrain a third time; US strikes Qeshm Island

Iran fired drones at a US radar installation in Bahrain, its third attack on American targets there in five days. Bahrain intercepted them and the US reported no damage or injuries. A US fighter then struck an Iranian Revolutionary Guard missile site on Qeshm Island in the Strait of Hormuz. The strikes ended a roughly two-month lull in a war that began Feb. 28 and is now in its eighth month. Qatar-led peace talks are stalled, and a US naval blockade is hurting Iran's economy.

  • A third US carrier group, the USS Theodore Roosevelt, has arrived, putting US naval strength above its level at the war's start.
  • Crisis Group analyst Azizi: the risk of renewed war now also depends on whether Tehran decides restraint costs more than escalation.
  • An IISS analyst says the Bahrain attacks are not yet a major escalation.

BBC World

Ukraine collapses part of the Crimean Bridge in a month-long planned operation

Ukraine severely damaged the Crimean Bridge. A large arched section fell into the water and the road is out of action. The rail section's arch is damaged but standing, and some rail services have resumed. Zelensky said the strike was the last stage of a seven-stage operation that systematically destroyed air defences and radars, using drones plus Flamingo and Neptune cruise missiles. Russia opened a terrorism case and says it will rebuild the bridge. The Crimea supply routes left are a land corridor through occupied Zaporizhzhia and Kherson, which Ukraine is also striking, and a ferry with very limited capacity.

  • Russia says it downed 2,256 Ukrainian drones, the largest attack of the war for the second day running.
  • Russian strikes damaged Kyiv's Dnipro bridges and heating and water systems overnight.
  • The timing is just before Thursday's Putin-Trump-Xi meeting in Shenzhen.

GTO Wizard: how to run custom solves with one bet size per board

GTO Wizard's anniversary stream shows how to set up your own postflop solves. Its "dynamic" sizing lets you give a few sizes, such as 40, 80 and 120% pot. The solver then picks one size per board: 120% on Q97, a small bet on K72. Its point is that flop sizing matters little. On one ace-queen-six board, the button's EV was 63.1% of the pot with a 33% bet and 63.4% with the larger, less-used size. You can compare sizes by duplicating the tab and checking the root node.

  • The most frequently used size is not always the highest-EV size.
  • Matching dynamic sizes to your online hotkeys makes study spots easier to play.

Lean people get sleep apnea too, and some fixes have trial support

Thomas DeLauer cites a study in Sleep finding that tongue fat is higher in people with apnea, independent of overall weight. Apnea and insulin resistance also feed each other. He says a Cochrane review of nine randomized trials (about 350 people) found that tongue and throat exercises (myofunctional therapy) cut breathing events substantially and reduced daytime sleepiness. Didgeridoo playing cut events by about 50%. Sleeping on your side helps, and alcohol and sedatives make apnea worse because they relax the airway. The video also promotes a honey brand and supplements, which are weakly supported.

  • Side-sleeping aids: a pillow between the legs, or a tennis ball in a sock taped to the shirt.
  • His suggested supplements: 3 g glycine and 200-400 mg theanine an hour before bed.

Daily Stoic reading · Judge each word and action

IV. Word after word, every one by itself, must the things that are spoken be conceived and understood; and so the things that are done, purpose after purpose, every one by itself likewise. And as in matter of purposes and actions, we must presently see what is the proper use and relation of every one; so of words must we be as ready, to consider of every one what is the true meaning, and signification of it according to truth and nature, however it be taken in common use.

— Marcus Aurelius, Meditations (tr. George Long)

Marcus is saying that you should take each statement and each action on its own terms, and check what a word actually means and what a move is actually for, rather than accepting the common usage or the default story. For a poker player this maps onto reviewing each decision in isolation: judge the spot in front of you by its own purpose and expected value, not by the results of the previous hand or the label the table has attached to it.

Mental model · Psychology

Social Proof

When uncertain, humans copy others. Munger cites the Jonestown massacre: once a few people drank the poison, social proof cascaded through 900. Less horrifically: laugh tracks work because we're wired to follow the crowd. The implication: in ambiguous situations, don't check what others are doing — check the underlying reality.
