Bessent opposes a half-point Fed hike as stocks and bitcoin fall

Treasury Secretary Bessent told CNBC the Fed should not raise rates by 50 basis points on Dec. 8-9. He cited the energy supply shock and lower crude prices. Governor Waller had said a half-point hike is probably needed because inflation is "spreading out." October minutes showed nearly all officials expect a quarter-point hike, and two floated a larger one. The Fed first hiked in September and held in October. Markets are under pressure: the Nasdaq fell more than 2% in morning trading, the VIX topped 30 (highest since March), and the 10-year yield fell to 4.95%. CME FedWatch shows a more than 98% chance of a quarter-point hike.

  • Citi expects a quarter-point hike, then a hold, then cuts later in 2027.
  • Bitcoin broke below $60,000 for the first time since July, heading for a seventh straight weekly loss.
  • CoinShares' base case is a $60k-$90k range through much of 2027, until falling inflation lets policy ease.

Use solver node-locking to find where the pool over-bluffs or under-bluffs

Thomas 21 says to look for spots where most players will be imbalanced because the correct strategy is hard to find at the table. In a low-stakes river spot on a four-flush board, the button has too few value hands, and most players do not know to check back about 90% of their trash. So they over-bluff. Node-locking the button to bluff more moved hero's king-queen from folding 43% to almost never folding. Hero should also check and let them bluff, rather than leading out. In an NL500 spot with a paired board and a third barrel, villain struggles to find enough folds, so bluff less. In a 35bb tournament spot where the lowjack check-raises and triple-barrels small, he argues a fold is good because the line has few natural bluffs. He also shows a big bet, a check, then another big bet on the river as a general pattern of broken aggression. He notes that a sound range analysis can still run into a ridiculous bluff.

  • Highlight the opponent's trash hands, then ask where their bluffs could come from.
  • Lock their bluff frequency higher or lower in the solver and compare the nodes to see the exploit.
  • Low stakes plus a hard-to-play board usually means over-bluffing or under-folding.

Daraxonrasib pill shrinks or stops advanced pancreatic tumours in early trial

In a trial of 61 patients with newly diagnosed, spread pancreatic cancer, the KRAS-inhibitor pill daraxonrasib stopped tumour growth in about 90%. Just over 60% had tumours shrink or disappear, and about 8% had tumours undetectable on CT. At one year, 76.6% were alive, versus the usual 40-45% with chemotherapy alone. Median follow-up was 18 months. Doctors say it is not yet a cure and that it is unknown how long the effect lasts. Longer studies are planned, and the drug may work in other cancers with similar KRAS mutations.

  • Presented at the EORTC-NCI-AACR symposium in Barcelona.
  • The comparison to chemotherapy survival is to historical figures, not a head-to-head arm.

Peter Attia

First treat-to-target vitamin D trial finds no drop in major cardiac events

Earlier large trials, such as VITAL, gave everyone the same dose, and many participants already had sufficient vitamin D. Those trials found no cardiovascular benefit. TARGET-D enrolled 630 people after a heart attack and titrated their vitamin D dose until 25(OH)D reached at least 40 ng/mL. Median baseline was 25 ng/mL. After about 4.2 years, major cardiovascular events were 15.7% versus 18.4% with usual care (HR 0.85, p=0.40), not significant. The trial was underpowered because COVID prevented full enrollment. New heart attacks were halved (3.8% vs 7.9%, HR 0.48, p=0.03), which drove the press headline. Attia's team is skeptical of that result. Heart attacks were a prespecified secondary outcome in VITAL and three other major trials, and none found an effect.

  • The 40 ng/mL target came from the authors' unpublished analysis, and the papers cited for it do not support it.
  • The heart-attack finding is a subgroup-style signal, not a confirmed effect.

Several studies found that a single high dose (at least 20 g, sometimes more) of creatine helped preserve cognitive function under sleep deprivation. The finding applies only to one day of sleep restriction, not days or weeks of it. The speaker treats it as an emergency option for a bad day, not a substitute for sleep.

Lean and Mathlib communities certify a proof of the Riemann Hypothesis

On November 20, 2026, Levent Alpöge released a proof of the Riemann Hypothesis as a Lean library of 17 million lines. The Lean and Mathlib communities say they have verified it. The statement matches Mathlib's own definition, compared with two independent tools. The library compiles for dozens of people. Several independent proof checkers accepted the exported proof, including two whose own soundness is formally proven. The checks ran on an inert export, so malicious code in the proof sources could not affect them. The statement does not say how the proof was produced. It says the mathematical community will now need to work out the new ideas in it.

  • Machine verification here does not depend on trusting human referees reading 17 million lines.

Daily Stoic reading · Judging events, not opinions

XXI. Wipe off all opinion stay the force and violence of unreasonable lusts and affections: circumscribe the present time examine whatsoever it be that is happened, either to thyself or to another: divide all present objects, either in that which is formal or material think of the last hour. That which thy neighbour hath committed, where the guilt of it lieth, there let it rest. Examine in order whatsoever is spoken. Let thy mind penetrate both into the effects, and into the causes. Rejoice thyself with true simplicity, and modesty; and that all middle things between virtue and vice are indifferent unto thee. Finally, love mankind; obey God.

— Marcus Aurelius, Meditations (tr. George Long)

In plain terms: drop your first opinions about what happened, keep the present moment small, and look at what actually occurred and why, whether it involved you or someone else. Leave other people's faults with them, and treat the things between virtue and vice as not worth emotional fuss. For poker, this maps onto separating the result of a hand from the decision that produced it, tracing outcomes back to their causes (villain's range, the board, the price offered) rather than to tilt-driven stories, and accepting that the variance of a single session is not a verdict on your play.
