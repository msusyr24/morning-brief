♠Poker

▶ GTO Wizard

▶

Turn solver aggregate flop reports into written rules of thumb, using SB vs CO 3-bet pot as the example

GTO Wizard's lesson uses aggregate reports, which show the solver's strategy across all 1,755 flops at once. Grouping by high card, suit and connectedness makes patterns visible. In SB vs CO 3-bet pots, SB c-bets 75% or more on Jack-high and above and under a third on Ten-high and below, because SB has the range advantage on high cards. SB also bets more on rainbow, paired and disconnected boards, and less on monotone and connected ones. The general principle is to attack more when fewer draws are available. When SB bets less often, it tends to use larger sizes. The advice is to write each finding into a spreadsheet so you retain it.

▶ Physionic

▶

Creatine may raise muscle strength even without exercise, but the new trial is mixed

A new four-arm randomized placebo-controlled trial compared creatine with and without exercise. On one strength measure creatine alone matched creatine plus exercise. On leg strength, creatine helped less than creatine plus exercise, and several other measures did not improve. The reviewer says the trial's statistics are weak. He says earlier studies, including ones in people in their 60s and 70s, mostly found independent strength effects. The two null studies had issues: one measured lean mass only, and the other was underpowered with 33 of 42 needed participants. This trial used up to 10 g a day, and side effects were generally absent, with one possible signal for bloating and constipation.

▶ Andrew Huberman

▶

How nicotine acts on dopamine, acetylcholine and norepinephrine, and why it is not a workout aid

Huberman says nicotine binds alpha4-beta2 receptors and drives three effects. It raises dopamine in the reward pathway, raises acetylcholine for focus (lasting about 30 to 45 minutes), and raises norepinephrine for alertness. It also suppresses appetite and lifts metabolism by roughly 2 to 5%, transiently. He says the combination of an alert brain with somewhat relaxed muscle suits cognitive work but is poor for physical performance. He says smokeless tobacco is linked to a 50-fold rise in mouth cancers. Smoking and vaping damage the blood-vessel lining.

₿Bitcoin & Macro

▶ Michael Saylor · Bitcoin Magazine

▶

Saylor: Strategy earns a spread on credit, and is shrinking STRC while credit markets contract

Saylor says Strategy makes money by issuing credit against its Bitcoin. The key yardstick is a hurdle rate of about 10.5%. If Bitcoin beats that, the gains go to common shareholders. If it only matches it, the result is neutral. Below it, the result is dilutive. He says net Bitcoin per share is the immediate accretion test, while gross Bitcoin per share only matters to long-horizon bulls. On STRC (12% dividend), he says credit markets have contracted for about six months. Demand has fallen from about $10.5B to perhaps $5-7.5B, so Strategy is buying back around $3B below par. He gives the cash runway as about 3.4 years of dollar reserves, and says the only existential risk is Bitcoin going to zero.

  • Capital structure cited: about $6.7B convertibles, $12.7B preferred, 850,000+ BTC
  • Swapping MSTR stock for BTC is accretive above roughly 1.3x mNAV; for dollars, above 1.0x
  • STRC buybacks fund a 'continuous auction' to find the natural size of demand at a 12% dividend
  • Dollar reserves cover about 3.4 years, versus 0.7 years when Bitcoin last hit $62,000

Which AI model is best depends on your domain, because progress follows training data

AI Explained argues that benchmark leadership flips by task: Gemini 4 Neon leads on PDF expert tasks, Fable 5.2 on frontier coding, and the aggregate indexes disagree. Daily Stoic reading · Acting justly, accepting what comes

XII. He hath got loose from the bonds of his body, and perceiving that within a very little while he must of necessity bid the world farewell, and leave all these things behind him, he wholly applied himself, as to righteousness in all his actions, so to the common nature in all things that should happen unto him. And contenting himself with these two things, to do all things justly, and whatsoever God doth send to like well of it: what others shall either say or think of him, or shall do against him, he doth not so much as trouble his thoughts with it. To go on straight, whither right and reason directed him, and by so doing to follow God, was the only thing that he did mind, that, his only business and occupation.

— Marcus Aurelius, Meditations (tr. George Long)

In plain terms: the person who has made peace with his own mortality stops spending energy on what others think or might do to him, and simply aims to act justly and accept whatever happens. For a poker player, that means judging each decision by whether the reasoning was sound at the moment it was made, and accepting that the cards and the opponents' choices are outside his control. Results are noisy over short samples, so a good process should be evaluated on its own terms, not on the outcome of any single hand or session.

Mental model · Psychology

Availability Bias

What's easily recalled feels more common. Plane crashes dominate the news, so flying feels more dangerous than driving — despite driving being ~100x deadlier per mile. Munger's defense: when estimating frequency, write down actual numbers, don't trust your gut's sense of 'how often this happens.'
