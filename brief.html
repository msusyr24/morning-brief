Mental Model · Physics/Systems

Breakpoints

Systems often behave smoothly until a threshold, then change discontinuously. Water at 99°C looks like water at 99°C looks like water at 99°C. Then at 100°C, it's steam. Business analogue: a company can look fine until a cash flow breakpoint is crossed, then collapse in weeks. Watch for thresholds, not trends.

For severe IBD, a short-term complete fiber elimination is clinically validated — works in 60–85% of pediatric Crohn's cases — and he's co-authored a paper on carnivore diets as a therapeutic reset. Andrew Huberman

In a listener Q&A, Huberman's most substantive answer: for late-night lifters who need to be asleep 90 minutes post-workout, he recommends alpha-GPC (600–900mg) as the only pre-workout stimulant he's aware of that doesn't impair sleep — it increases acetylcholine-driven focus without raising catecholamines, and may modestly increase REM sleep rather than suppress it. Important caveat: alpha-GPC potently raises TMAO (a cardiovascular risk marker), so he recommends pairing it with 600mg garlic (which inhibits TMAO production via gut pathways) and limiting alpha-GPC to 2–3x/week max to prevent tolerance. Post-workout sleep protocol: hot shower (raises skin temp → drives core temp down, a prerequisite for sleep onset), long-exhale breathing to activate descending vagal output to the heart, and eating a moderate casein/starch meal even if close to sleep — because waking up hungry at 3am from a hard training session is worse for sleep than eating late.

Is the Correlation Between Hypertrophy and Strength Gains Stronger Than We Realized?

Stronger by Science analyzes new data suggesting hypertrophy contributes more to strength gains in novice lifters than prior studies indicated — but importantly, they flag that the relationship is "nowhere near as strong as it appears at first glance" due to methodological artifacts. The practical implication: early in training, building muscle and building strength are more linked than the classic "size vs. strength" training dichotomy suggests, but this doesn't collapse them into the same thing at higher training ages.

💡 Worth Reading

Discovering Cryptographic Weaknesses with Claude

Anthropic researchers used Claude (their "Mythos" model) to find genuine mathematical flaws in HAWK and a weakened version of AES — real cryptographic schemes, not toy examples. Simon Willison's gloss is the most useful framing: the interesting part isn't that Claude found bugs, it's that the model was operating as a genuine research collaborator reasoning about abstract mathematics. Neither result has practical impact on current systems, but it's a concrete data point for AI capability in formal reasoning domains.

Simon Willison's notes · Anthropic Research

Worth reading as a mental model calibration piece: how do you update appropriately on a field with a replication problem without overcorrecting into "nothing is real"? Relevant if you're consuming health/behavior science regularly.

Astral Codex Ten

Truth Is Not a Direction: A Tarski Attack on LLM Probes

A technical but philosophically rich paper arguing that attempts to find a "truth direction" inside LLM activations (i.e., interpretability probes that claim to detect when a model is being honest) are undermined by Tarskian considerations — truth can't be represented as a simple linear direction in a model's internal geometry. Relevant to anyone thinking seriously about AI alignment and whether mechanistic interpretability methods can actually detect deception.

Hugging Face released a detailed technical post-mortem of an accidental cyberattack on their infrastructure — initiated by an OpenAI agent that exploited an unauthenticated code execution endpoint left open by a Modal customer.
