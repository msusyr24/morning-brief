🧬 Health & Science

Insulin Resistance as Primary Cardiac Risk — Ignored in Favor of LDL

From the Thomas DeLauer / Dr. Anthony J. interview: a study of 28,000 early-onset heart attack patients found insulin resistance had a hazard ratio of 6.4x — versus LDL at 1.38x and hypertension at 2.3x. The mechanistic claim is that the insulin-resistant heart loses its preferred fuel (fats) and begins absorbing lipids into arterial walls, with cholesterol appearing in autopsies as a consequence rather than a cause. The cardiologist argues the PCSK9 drug category (which lowers LDL and LP(a)) showed higher cardiac death rates vs. placebo in a reanalysis of the FOURIER trial, though this finding remains contested.

Fructose Cleared by Small Intestine First — Not the Liver

A 2018 finding (confirmed in follow-up human research) overturns the prevailing Lustig model: the small intestine clears ~90% of fructose at low doses, converting it to glucose and lactate, with the liver acting as overflow backup. The dose threshold is estimated at 10–25g fructose per sitting in humans, with liquid sugar on an empty stomach hitting saturation fastest (~10g, roughly half a can of soda). Above the threshold, the liver handles overflow via lipogenesis — so the old mechanism isn't wrong, just the anatomy of where it starts. Fructose remains harmful via separate pathways: blunted ghrelin response, absent insulin/satiety signal, and heightened craving for additional food vs. glucose.

Peter Attia Revises Views on Ketosis and Extended Fasting

Attia now believes keto works through satiety and energy intake control — not insulin suppression specifically — and that energy imbalance is the root cause of metabolic dysfunction (hyperinsulinemia is a fellow-traveler, not the driver). On extended fasting: he ran quarterly 7–10 day fasts for three years targeting autophagy, but has since stopped, citing insufficient human evidence that autophagy benefits in animal models translate meaningfully and questioning whether the muscle cost is worth it. His current position: dietary strategy matters less than hitting minimum protein (1.4g/kg), and he's agnostic on the vehicle.
