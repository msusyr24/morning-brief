Espen Jorstad reviews WCOOP $10k Main final table short-stack ICM spotsvideo

Jorstad walks through final table hands with GTO Wizard. His main point is that bet sizing changes the solver's strategy. A small 3-bet to 5.5bb gives the opener a good price, so the solver peels wide (even A9o) and jams A-K suited more. A bigger 3-bet to 7bb produces far less calling and more jam-or-fold. He thinks players over-bluff 3-bets and 4-bets at final tables. That makes hands like A5s and AJo more attractive as jams against them. Risk premiums were 6.4% to 6.9% for the two short stacks and 11.3% in an early-position confrontation.

  • Smaller 3-bet sizes make the opener call more and make you jam A-K suited more often.
  • In ICM, calling ranges shrink when you are covered, especially early position versus early position.
  • Jorstad suggests building open-jam ranges with the smallest pairs, which surprised him when the solver jammed almost no pairs.
