Zelensky says Starshield speed limits are hobbling Ukraine's drone interceptors

Zelensky says speed limits of about 100mph (161km/h) on Starshield, the military version of Starlink that the US government provides to Ukraine, are still in place in Russian-occupied areas. Interceptor drones lose contact with their operators at that speed and cannot catch Russian jet-powered Shahed drones aimed at electricity substations. He asked the US and Musk to lift the limits, and said imposing them there amounts to partly recognising occupied territory as not Ukrainian. Musk and Trump have given conflicting accounts of who is responsible. Russia hit energy and rail sites across several regions overnight, and Ukraine says it struck a refinery and a petrochemical plant in Nizhnekamsk, Tatarstan.

  • Estonia will close its border crossings with Russia from 1 December after a suspected GRU-ordered arson attempt on drone maker Threod Systems; eight people were detained.
  • Ukraine also faces a shortage of US-made Patriot interceptors.

Player walks out of a dorm-lobby 5/10/25/50 game on credit, suspecting a setup

An experienced home-game player lost his $1k buy-in in about 90 minutes at a game in a student dorm lobby. Red flags he lists: cheap dice chips, no cut card, hand shuffling, the bottom card visible on most deals, no rake, play entirely on credit, and 20-year-olds casually reloading $2k to $8k. His theory is that the group could erase its internal debts and split what it won from outsiders. He admits he has no evidence of this. His main lesson is that he saw every warning sign and still sat down.

  • Check the setup and the settlement method before you buy in.

Valter Longo: fasting-mimicking diet may spare muscle where water-only fasting does not

Longo says the five-day fasting-mimicking diet is in roughly 50 clinical trials. It starts at about 1,100 calories on day 1, then about 770 a day, and is high in fat, low in sugar and low in protein. He says six or seven trials found no significant lean-mass loss. In one Tennessee trial, a Mediterranean diet cost overweight participants about 5 pounds of lean mass over seven months. He cites an Italian trial with six monthly cycles that showed lean-mass gain, but he cautions that it used bioimpedance rather than DEXA. He proposes that muscle shrinks during the fast, then rebuilds through stem-cell activation. He calls that mechanism unproven for strength. He also says water-only fasting increased gut leakiness in mice, while the prebiotic-rich diet raised Lactobacillus threefold. This is a long interview and includes a sponsor read.

  • Insulin resistance and A1C improvements are described as very clear in the data.
  • Ketones reach only about 1-2 mmol by design.
  • The gut and Crohn's findings come from mice plus Stanford and Miami clinical studies.

Seneca advised reading authors you disagree with, and he quoted Epicurus, head of a rival school, more than any other philosopher. Daily Stoic reading · Audit your own mind

XI. What is the use that now at this present I make of my soul? Thus from time to time and upon all occasions thou must put this question to thyself; what is now that part of mine which they call the rational mistress part, employed about? Whose soul do I now properly possess? a child's? or a youth's? a woman's? or a tyrant's? some brute, or some wild beast's soul?

— Marcus Aurelius, Meditations (tr. George Long)

Marcus tells himself to regularly stop and ask what his reasoning faculty is actually busy with, and what kind of character currently runs his thinking: a child's impulsiveness, a tyrant's appetite, or an animal's reactions. For a poker player, this is a mid-session check on whether you are still playing the plan or have shifted into tilt, chasing losses, or punishing a villain out of ego. The discipline is to ask the question on every decision, not just after a bad beat.

Mental model · Psychology

Incentive-Caused Bias

Munger on FedEx: the company couldn't get packages sorted on time until they stopped paying workers by the hour and started paying by the shift. Workers had been incentivized to drag out the job. Once the incentive flipped, the bottleneck vanished overnight. Whenever a system produces strange behavior, ask: what is it paying for?
