🧬 Health & Science

Is the Correlation Between Hypertrophy and Strength Gains Stronger Than We Realized?

Stronger by Science digs into whether muscle growth actually drives strength gains, and finds the relationship is more nuanced than prior studies suggested: in novice lifters, hypertrophy likely accounts for more of the strength gain than previously estimated, but the correlation is still far from deterministic — neural adaptations, technique, and other factors remain significant contributors. The practical implication is that chasing hypertrophy and chasing strength are more aligned in early training phases than the field previously gave credit for, but diverge significantly as lifters advance.
