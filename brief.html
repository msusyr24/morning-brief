How to attack limpers from the blinds, according to solver work

Solvers rarely limp in standard 100bb cash games because limping needs about 40% pot equity to break even (risk 1 into a 2.5bb pot), which equals facing a 2x pot bet. Raising does better. Aces make about 10bb by raising versus 8.54bb by limping, and raising weak hands adds fold equity and avoids rake. Against a recreational limper, the small blind should overlimp with connected hands like 76s, which can win a stack by making straights. From the big blind, raise to 3-4x the pot, about 7.5-10bb out of position, and use a linear range of medium pairs and up, suited broadways, some suited aces and connectors.

  • Against passive, wide-calling limpers, go linear and top-heavy: strong hands plus hands with good board coverage
  • Skip low offsuit and weak suited hands, because ace-x in their range will not fold
  • Against a solver-like limper, the balanced big-blind range is polarized and checks Q-Q, J-J, T-T and A-K to slowplay

California law bars under-18s from buying muscle-building and weight-loss supplements

Renaissance Periodization says AB 2030 takes effect January 1, 2027. It restricts under-18 sales of supplements marketed for muscle building or weight loss, and creatine is named in it. Whey protein is likely covered depending on marketing and store placement. Sellers must check age, including online, with fines up to $500 per violation. RP argues the supporting evidence is correlational: diet-pill users had about 5.6 times the odds of a later eating disorder, and teen protein users were about twice as likely to report later steroid use. RP also says no evidence shows creatine, whey or caffeine harms teens, and that relabeling a product avoids the law.

◎Mind & Philosophy

Figs in Winter (Pigliucci)

Pigliucci: Stoic 'indifferents' are not indifference to everything

Pigliucci revisits one of the most misunderstood Stoic concepts. The early Stoic terms are adiaphora (neither good nor bad in themselves), proēgmena (preferred) and apoproēgmena (dispreferred), which Cicero translated as indifferentia, praeposita and reiecta. He notes Cicero's objection in De Finibus book 4: that this is just Aristotle's view with different words, which would make Stoicism a semantic variant of Aristotelianism. Pigliucci finds the concept clear and practically useful, and wants to defend it against that charge.

Study: frontier AI now designs political-science research better than a top expert

Andy Hall and Andy Myers report that recent frontier models consistently propose research designs that both human and LLM graders rate as more compelling than those a leading expert in the field was developing. The research questions the AI chose, however, were rated substantially less valuable than leading political-science research. So AI is strong at method and still weak at deciding what is worth studying.

Daily Stoic reading · Endings are not harm

XIX. Of an operation and of a purpose there is an ending, or of an action and of a purpose we say commonly, that it is at an end: from opinion also there is an absolute cessation, which is as it were the death of it. In all this there is no hurt. Apply this now to a man's age, as first, a child; then a youth, then a young man, then an old man; every change from one age to another is a kind of death And all this while here no matter of grief yet. Pass now unto that life first, that which thou livedst under thy grandfather, then under thy mother, then under thy father. And thus when through the whole course of thy life hitherto thou hast found and observed many alterations, many changes, many kinds of endings and cessations, put this question to thyself What matter of grief or sorrow dost thou find in any of these? Or what doest thou suffer through any of these? If in none of these, then neither in the ending and consummation of thy whole life, which is also but a cessation and change.

— Marcus Aurelius, Meditations (tr. George Long)

Every action, purpose, and stage of life comes to an end, and Marcus argues that a change which is really just an ending is not in itself a loss to grieve over. If you look back at the shifts you have already lived through and find nothing there that wounded you, the same reasoning applies to the final ending of your life. For a poker player, this points to treating the close of each session, each bankroll swing, or each run of luck as a transition to be assessed on its merits rather than a verdict to be mourned, which keeps tilt from taking hold after a bad beat.
