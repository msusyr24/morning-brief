Contrast Misreaction

We judge things relative to recent comparisons, not absolute values. A $2,000 suit feels cheap after you've committed to a $30,000 car. Real estate agents show you a worse house first. Worth your time:

Nick Norwitz

Norwitz did 30 days on the strictest version of carnivore — the "lion diet" (beef, salt, water only) — and reports daily bowel movements and near-perfect gut function, consistent with a 2012 World Journal of Gastroenterology study showing that fiber elimination moved IBS-C patients from one BM per 3.75 days to daily, with bloating and straining eliminated. He and a collaborator published a case series of 10 medically-confirmed IBD patients (Crohn's, ulcerative colitis) who experienced "life-changing" improvements on carnivore, and cites a Cell Host & Microbe study showing fiber-free diets starve off pathological gut bacteria like Mucispirillum. On nutrients: calcium is a real concern on lion diet (muscle meat has ~10-20mg/100g vs. RDA of 1,000mg), and his omega-3 index dropped from 15.68% to 10.51% in 30 days — a meaningful decline he considers disqualifying for long-term lion diet given his ApoE4 status. The vitamin C paradox is the most interesting finding: glucose and vitamin C compete for the same cellular transporters, so low-carb diets may dramatically reduce vitamin C requirements, and a ketogenic state may regenerate vitamin C via the NADPH system — which is likely why Stefansson survived a supervised all-meat year in 1928 without scurvy.

Video: The Strange Science of Eating Only Meat

Jared Alderman

Alderman talks about the solver trap directly from personal experience: he went on a 600-hour break-even stretch at 5/10/20 because he was applying GTO balance logic against a nit reg with 8% PFR and opening ATo as a three-bet bluff to "stay balanced." His core point is that creative, exploitative poker — especially at deep stacks (300-400bb) — is what makes live cash games distinct and enjoyable, and that treating live games like online equilibrium exercises is both EV-negative and soul-crushing. He eventually snapped out of it when he refocused on the player in front of him rather than the solver output. The GTO Lab short reinforces this with a multiway bluff concept: a bluff gets cleaner when players behind can't punish it, so the key calculation is opponent-specific read + multiway position, not pure frequency.

Videos: The Problem With Poker Solvers · GTO Lab: Bluff Multiway

Peter Attia (with Michael Davidson)

Davidson, a lipidologist at University of Chicago whose father died of a heart attack at 47 (with Davidson age 16), makes the primordial prevention case with a memorable framework: the "8 gram rule" — 200mg/dL LDL × 40 years = 8g lifetime LDL exposure, which is the threshold associated with atherosclerosis. Below 80mg/dL sustained lifelong, heart disease effectively doesn't occur. He contrasts his own trajectory (statin from medical school onward) against his brother's (went vegetarian, no statin until late, bypass at 44), calling it the most instructive n-of-1 he knows. Both Attia and Davidson argue the core problem is that LDL isn't treated like smoking or hypertension — causally, not probabilistically — and that 10-year risk calculators are the wrong framework entirely. Davidson also flags CETPi inhibitors (specifically obicetrapib, his company's drug) as potentially the last major LDL-lowering mechanism needed to close the case on aggressive early treatment.

Video: 407 — Preventing Cardiovascular and Alzheimer's Disease

🧬 Health & Science

Primordial Prevention: The Case for Treating LDL in Your 30s

The Peter Attia / Michael Davidson conversation is worth pulling out here for the specific framework. Davidson's "8 gram rule" is a useful mental model: cumulative lifetime LDL exposure (mg/dL × years) predicts cardiovascular disease, and keeping LDL below 80mg/dL lifelong appears to prevent it almost entirely. His personal n-of-1 — himself (statin from medical school, no events) vs. his brother (vegetarian, delayed statin, bypass at 44) — is a compelling illustration of the early-treatment argument. The key reframe Attia credits to Allan Sniderman: stop calculating 10-year risk and instead treat causal drivers, the same logic applied to smoking cessation regardless of pack-year history.

GLUT4 and Post-Meal Glucose Clearance

DeLauer's GLUT4 video has legitimate mechanistic content worth extracting. Muscle contraction activates GLUT4 transporters via an insulin-independent pathway (AMPK activation), and critically, the effect lingers — a cardiology study of type 2 diabetics showed glucose infusion rate jumped from 3.4 to 6.6 mg/kg/min (216% increase) after brief moderate exercise and was still elevated at 4.8 thirty minutes post-exercise. The practical protocol: compound movements (thrusters, lunges-to-press) recruiting large muscle groups within 15-30 min of eating; food-order manipulation (protein → fiber → carbs) to slow gastric emptying; and consistent training builds 52% more GLUT4 protein in trained muscle (leg extension study, 15-hour sustained insulin sensitivity improvement). The apple cider vinegar mechanism (acetic acid slows gastric emptying, compounds the effect) and resistant starches (cooled rice/potato) stack logically on top.

Carnivore and Gut Health: Specific Data Points from Norwitz

Three findings worth tracking: (1) A 2012 study in World Journal of Gastroenterology found a dose-dependent worsening of IBS-C with increasing fiber intake — the more fiber, the worse the constipation. (2) A Cell Host & Microbe study found fiber-free diets starve pathological gut bacteria (specifically Mucispirillum) via intraluminal migration changes. (3) A microbiome diversity study comparing 10 long-term carnivore dieters (avg 3 years) against 874 matched controls found no significant difference in diversity metrics — directly challenging the assumption that fiber is required for microbiome health. The calcium gap on a dairy-free lion diet is real and mathematically stark: muscle meat provides ~10-20mg calcium per 100g against an RDA of 1,000-1,200mg, requiring ~11 lbs of beef daily to hit target.
