Mental Model · Physics/Economics

Equilibrium

Systems settle into balances where forces cancel. Markets find price equilibrium; ecosystems find predator-prey balance; workplaces find culture equilibrium. Shifting equilibrium requires changing underlying forces, not fighting symptoms. Munger applies this to regulation: treat symptoms and you'll return to equilibrium; change incentives and the new equilibrium shifts.

🧬 Health & Science

Is the Correlation Between Hypertrophy and Strength Gains Stronger Than We Realized?

Stronger by Science digs into whether muscle size gains actually drive strength gains — and finds a more nuanced answer than prior research suggested. The correlation between hypertrophy and strength is stronger in new lifters than previously thought, but the overall relationship is still far from deterministic: neural adaptations, skill, and other factors dominate, especially in trained individuals. The takeaway for programming: don't assume hypertrophy work is just "bodybuilder stuff" separate from strength — in early training especially, they're deeply linked — but also don't assume getting bigger guarantees getting stronger.
