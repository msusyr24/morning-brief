Natto lowers post-meal blood sugar and may cut stroke risk, but the evidence has gaps

Short randomized meal trials found that natto lowered blood glucose compared with rice alone and with a control meal. It also lowered insulin compared with the control meal. Those trials were mostly designed to test food viscosity, so natto cannot be isolated as the cause. Large Japanese cohort studies show lower stroke risk in the highest natto-intake group. Unfermented soy protein did not show the same benefit, which suggests natto itself matters. The caveats are that all the studies were in Japan and that diet was measured only once, at baseline.

  • Studies linked cardiovascular benefit to 7 to 30 g of natto a day, and as little as 7 g was effective.
  • The host speculates that gamma-PGA, the sticky component, drives the benefit, so stickier natto may be better.
  • Evidence for lower cholesterol is suggestive and unconfirmed.

Huberman: four conditions for falling asleep, centered on a falling core temperature

Falling asleep requires core body temperature to drop and heart rate to slow. You can cool the room or have a hot shower or bath, which leads to a drop in core temperature afterward. Long, deliberate exhales slow the heart rate. A racing mind can't be stopped, but you can shift attention to a sound or to bodily sensations. The fourth condition, forgetting the position of your limbs, was cut off in the text.

Bitcoin Magazine

Strategic Bitcoin Reserve bill clears House committee, awaits floor vote

H.R. 8957, the American Reserve Modernization Act, was reported out of House Financial Services after a 28-21 committee vote. It has 23 cosponsors, including Democrat Jared Golden. Treasury would have to set up a Strategic Bitcoin Reserve and a separate Digital Asset Stockpile within 180 days. Reserve bitcoin could not be sold, swapped or encumbered for 20 years. After that, Treasury could recommend selling at most 10% in any two-year period. The reserve exists today only under a March 2025 executive order, which a future president could reverse.

  • The committee removed the original funding ideas: Fed remittances, revaluing the Fed's gold certificates, tariff revenue and tax payments.
  • The amended text bars borrowing, collateral, new taxes or deficit spending to buy bitcoin.
  • An annual Proof of Reserve report would be independently audited, with GAO oversight.
  • The bill has not had a floor vote, and the Senate would still have to act.

Anthropic and Claude produce a Lean-verified proof of the Riemann Hypothesis

Anthropic released a Lean 4 proof of the Riemann Hypothesis and the Generalized Riemann Hypothesis for Dirichlet L-functions. It builds on Levent Alpöge's proof, written with Claude, and uses only the standard Lean axioms. The readable version is about 300,000 lines and the compact one about 76,000. The repository says the proof has passed machine checks only. No mathematician has read it through, and no paper accompanies it. Treat it as a claim awaiting human scrutiny.

Daily Stoic reading · Knowing when enough is enough

The body is to everyone the proper measure of its possessions, as the foot is of the shoe. If, therefore, you stop at this, you will keep the measure; but if you move beyond it, you must necessarily be carried forward, as down a precipice; as in the case of a shoe, if you go beyond its fitness to the foot, it comes first to be gilded, then purple, and then studded with jewels. For to that which once exceeds the fit measure there is no bound.

— Epictetus, Enchiridion

Epictetus says your body sets a natural size for what you need, the way a foot sets the size of a shoe, and that once you go past that size there is no natural stopping point, so each addition leads to another. For a poker player and bettor, the same applies to bankroll, bet sizing and stakes: a size that fits your edge and your tolerance for swings is the right measure, and every step beyond it, such as chasing losses or moving up in stakes too fast, tends to pull you further without a built-in brake.

Mental model · Math/Statistics

Base Rates

When evaluating any individual case, start with the frequency across the population. Restaurants fail ~60% in year one — no matter how passionate your friend's pitch is, that's your prior. Doctors miss this constantly: a positive test for a rare disease is usually a false positive, even if the test is 95% accurate. Anchor on the base rate before adjusting for specifics.
