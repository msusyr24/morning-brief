◆AI Capability1

Google releases Gemini 4 Hydrogen, a fast, cheaper model that scores above its larger sibling

Google says Hydrogen scores 51 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index at default settings, against 50 for the larger Argon, and 54 at its highest setting. Pricing is $1.50 per million input tokens and $7.50 per million output tokens, flat across a 1 million-token context. Versus Gemini 3.8 Flash it is 2.5 times faster, Terminal-Bench 4.1 scores rise from 18.2% to 57%, and fabricated claims drop by more than 5x. Google also introduced Orchestrator in Antigravity, which switches between Carbon and Hydrogen in one session. On Terminal-Bench 4.0 it reached near-Carbon success with 24% less quota and 19% faster completion. These are Google's own figures.

  • Four thinking levels: low, medium, high (default), and new xhigh
  • Handles up to 1M tokens of PDFs, spreadsheets, audio and video, with up to 1M output tokens
  • Demos include a slide deck built from a keynote video and an animated pixel canvas in Google Sheets

◈Romania3

Lukoil's Petrotel refinery in Ploiești plans to restart if crude arrives by Nov. 20

Petrotel, about 20% of Romania's fuel production, has been idle since October 2025 and is the only one of the four refineries still shut. It has been under state supervision since February after US sanctions on Lukoil. A Kazakh crude shipment is due at Constanța by November 20, and the company says it complies with EU, US and UK sanctions. The cogeneration plant is already running. Without crude the restart cannot continue, and the refinery may return to long-term conservation.

  • The refinery is in insolvency proceedings, which the company says protect the asset and jobs

Romania Insider

Cernavodă nuclear units return after summer shutdown, with Unit 1 at full power Nov. 14

Unit 2 was synced to the grid on November 11. Unit 1 is delayed by remedial work and is due at nominal power on November 14. Each unit is 700 MW, and together they provide about 18% of Romania's electricity. Low Danube levels, which cut cooling water, forced the shutdowns in July and August. The outage increased evening import dependence and raised spot prices, which are increasingly showing in consumer bills.

  • The company gave no details on the nature of the remedial work

Romania's 2026 Christmas market dates: Timișoara, Sibiu, Cluj, Alba Iulia, Oradea and others

Bucharest runs Nov. 27 to Dec. 27 and Oradea Nov. 27 to Dec. 26, while Brașov has a preview on Nov. 27 and opens officially Nov. 30.
